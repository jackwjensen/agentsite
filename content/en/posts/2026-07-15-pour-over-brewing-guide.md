A pour-over is the simplest way to taste what a coffee actually is: no
pressure, no milk, nowhere to hide. It is also the easiest brew to get
slightly wrong every day for years. Three variables do most of the work.

## 1. Water temperature

Aim for 92–96 °C. Water straight off the boil scalds light roasts; water
that has stood for five minutes under-extracts them. Thirty seconds off the
boil is the practical sweet spot.

## 2. Grind size

Medium-fine, like coarse sand. If the brew runs through in under two
minutes, grind finer; over four, grind coarser. The clock is a better guide
than the grinder's numbers.

## 3. Patience

Pour in stages: a small bloom pour, a rest, then slow spirals. Total brew
time around three minutes for a standard cup.
