## What this site collects

Nothing. The core of this site sets **no cookies**, runs **no analytics**,
loads **no third-party fonts, scripts or trackers**, and stores no personal
data about visitors.

## Server logs

The web server keeps short-lived technical logs (IP address, requested URL,
user agent) for operating and securing the site. They are size-capped and
rotate automatically; they are not used to identify visitors.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and erase personal data
held about you. Since this site stores none, there is normally nothing to
request — but you can always write to the address in the footer.
