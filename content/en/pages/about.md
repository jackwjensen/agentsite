## What a page like this is for

An about page introduces the people or company behind the site. When your AI
assistant sets up your own site, it interviews you and rewrites this page with
your real story.

## How it works underneath

This page is a single markdown file per language: `content/en/pages/about.md`
and `content/da/pages/about.md`. Same filename, same URL slug — only the
language prefix differs. It is also readable by AI agents as raw markdown at
[/about.md](/about.md).
